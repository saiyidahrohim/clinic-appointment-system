# Requirements

## Functional requirements (what the system must DO)

1. The system shall allow patients to register and log in using their personal information and account credentials.
2. The system shall allow patients to book appointments by selecting a doctor, appointment date, and available time slot.
3. The system shall allow patients to view their appointment details, including the doctor’s name, date, time, and appointment status.
4. The system shall allow patients to cancel or reschedule their appointments.
5. The system shall allow clinic receptionists to view, confirm, update, and manage patient appointments.

## Non-Functional Requirements (How the system must PERFORM)

1. Performance: The system shall display appointment search results within 2 seconds under normal operating conditions.
2. Security: The system shall protect patient information by requiring secure login and restricting access to authorized users.
3. Usability: The system shall provide a simple, user-friendly interface that allows patients to book an appointment within 5 minutes without assistance.
